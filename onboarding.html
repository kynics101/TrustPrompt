Welcome to TrustPrompt

TrustPrompt checks what you type in ChatGPT or Claude and warns you if it spots personal or sensitive information, before you send it.

Step 1 of 3

What the badge colors mean

The badge appears near the prompt box.

  • Gray: Waiting or still typing
  • Green: No detected privacy risk
  • Yellow: Low risk
  • Orange: Moderate risk (see the side panel)
  • Red: High risk (see the side panel and masked version)

What to know

  • It can see your typing. While it is on, TrustPrompt reads everything in the ChatGPT or Claude prompt box, including text you have not sent or have deleted. It stops when you pause it, decline it, or uninstall it.
  • Checking happens on your device. TrustPrompt does not send your text anywhere and does not save your prompts.
  • It can miss things. "No Detected Privacy Risk" does not mean your prompt is safe.
  • You decide. TrustPrompt only advises. You can always send your original prompt.
  • Sending is final. Once you send a prompt, TrustPrompt cannot take it back. Your own OpenAI or Anthropic account rules apply, and free-tier prompts may be kept or used for training.

Your choice

If you're in the study

Use made-up details only. Do not click "Send Anyway." Just watch the badge and side panel, then answer the questionnaire. Full details are in the Informed Consent Form.

How to uninstall

Open your browser's extensions page, find TrustPrompt, and click Remove. Do this too if it stops working properly.

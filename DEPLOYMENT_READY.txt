================================================================================
  KNOWN LIMITATIONS
================================================================================

1. Obfuscated Keys
   - Keys split across lines may not be detected
   - Base64-encoded credentials need preprocessing
   - URL-encoded credentials may need decoding

2. Custom Formats
   - Non-standard key formats require labelled format
   - Minimum 20 characters for unlabelled detection

3. Very Short Keys
   - Keys <20 characters may not match
   - Rare in practice (most vendors use 20+ chars)

4. Context Awareness
   - Cannot distinguish between real and documentation examples
   - Relies on entropy filtering (3.5 bits/char minimum)

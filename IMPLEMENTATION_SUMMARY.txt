DETECTION LOGIC
═════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════

Path B (Gazetteer) - 3 Decision Steps:
──────────────────────────────────────

  STEP 1: Detect term in gazetteer word lists
          ↓
  STEP 2: Extract context (±150 characters)
          ↓
  STEP 3: Analyze context
          
          If RISKY markers found:
            - Personal pronouns (I'm, my, we)
            - Possession + disclosure
            - Relationship + sensitivity
            - Contact intent
            ↓ FLAG AS PII
          
          Else if SAFE markers found:
            - Questions (what, how, explain)
            - Education (language, term, grammar)
            - Food/culture references
            - Measurements
            ↓ ALLOW (don't flag)
          
          Else:
            ↓ ALLOW (default safe)


Path A (Regex) - 3 Decision Steps:
──────────────────────────────────

  STEP 1: Match number to phone pattern
          ↓
  STEP 2: Extract context (±100 characters)
          ↓
  STEP 3: Check for measurement markers
          
          If conversion action found (convert, turn, into)
          AND unit keyword found (grams, meters, watts, etc.):
            ↓ FILTER AS SAFE (don't flag)
          
          Else if unit found after number:
            ↓ FILTER AS SAFE (don't flag)
          
          Else:
            ↓ FLAG AS RISKY PII

SEMANTIC MARKERS
═════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════

RISKY Markers (Flag as PII):
────────────────────────────
- Personal pronouns: i'm, i am, my, our
- Medical possession: i have [condition]
- Possession + being: my girlfriend is
- Contact actions: create email with, send my
- Relationship + intent: friend is + understand
- Self-identity: as a X + vulnerable/challenge context

SAFE Markers (Allow without flagging):
──────────────────────────────────────
- Questions: what, how, explain, describe, can you
- Education: language, term, grammar, translate, pronunciation
- Food/culture: cuisine, food, restaurant, culture, history, tradition
- Measurements: grams, kg, meters, seconds, watts, bytes
- Conversions: convert, turn, transform, into, to
